## Setup

:::definition[Heat kernel]{#def-heat-kernel}
The heat kernel on $\R$ is
$$
\begin{equation}
    K_t(x) = \df{1}{\sqrt{4\pi t}} e^{-x^2/(4t)}, \qquad t > 0.
\end{equation}
$$
:::
